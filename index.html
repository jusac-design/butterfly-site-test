Amy's story

From the classroom to her own cleaning business, and Butterfly.

Amy switched from a career in teaching to a small, successful cleaning business.

My dad could see I had the itch the summer before. School holidays were too long not to be doing something meaningful, so I left teaching and just did it. My family and friends were so supportive.

I have amazing clients and I grew really quickly. Soon, keeping track of weekly, fortnightly and monthly cleans was really hard. Add promoting yourself on social media, like every small business owner has to, and it was all quite intense.

So I created my Butterfly App. It cut my admin right down, so I can get on with the job I love. Moving clients around is a breeze, and it keeps a record of every mile, even the odd run for supplies.

“I spend far less time on admin. It pretty much does it all for me.”

Your journey, your miles, all recorded for you.

Your day starts and ends at home. At the end of the day, Butterfly works out the miles for every journey and keeps your mileage log ready for your tax return.

Butterfly tipBusiness miles can lower your tax bill.

HMRC lets you claim 55p a mile for your first 10,000 business miles each tax year, then 25p a mile. It comes off the profit you pay tax on, and Butterfly keeps the log for you.

Your week, money and tax year at a glance. Butterfly keeps your income, expenses and miles as digital records to help you prepare for Making Tax Digital, ready to export for your accountant, Xero or QuickBooks.

Butterfly tipMaking Tax Digital is arriving in stages.

If your income from self-employment (and any property) is over £50,000, you've needed it since 6 April 2026. Over £30,000 joins from 6 April 2027, and over £20,000 from 6 April 2028. It counts your income before expenses, not your profit. You keep digital records and send HMRC a short update every quarter, using compatible software. Check if it applies to you on GOV.UK

Questions

Good to know.

Where do I start?

Four quick steps and you’re ready to fly:

  1. In Settings, add your bank details, working hours and price list.
  2. Add your clients.
  3. Add your cleans to the Diary.
  4. Send your first reminder by WhatsApp, text or another app. Butterfly writes it for you.

You’ll find the same guide in the app, under Settings, then Help.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Sign up and use everything free for 30 days. You only set up a payment if you decide to stay.

What happens when my free trial ends?

Choose your plan before your 30 days are up and it’s only £9.95 a month, for as long as you stay. If you don’t, Butterfly turns read-only and nothing is lost: your records are kept safe for 90 days, so you can download them or join whenever you’re ready.

How do I pay?

By monthly Direct Debit through GoCardless, protected by the Direct Debit Guarantee. There’s no VAT to add, and you can cancel any time.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. You keep using Butterfly until the end of the month you’ve paid for, and you can download all your data to take with you.

Can I bring my existing bookings with me?

Yes. Bring your current calendar in once from a calendar file (Google, Apple or Outlook), and your Butterfly bookings can show in your phone or desktop calendar too.

Is my clients’ information safe?

Your records are yours and only you can see them. Key-safe codes never appear in a message or a download, and the Butterfly team can only look at your account if you let them.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Butterfly works in your phone’s web browser and you can add it to your home screen, so it opens just like an app. It works on a tablet or computer too.

Is Butterfly only for cleaners?

Butterfly started with cleaning, because that’s Amy’s world. Mobile hairdressers and beauty therapists are next, and we’d love to hear from you if that’s you.

How do I get help?

Message us from inside the app, or email hello@butterflyapp.co.uk. Our UK team replies within 1 working day.
